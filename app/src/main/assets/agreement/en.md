## 1. What VowiDroid is

VowiDroid is an independent, unofficial WiFi Calling client. It uses a SIM card you hold to connect to your mobile carrier's Wi-Fi calling service, so that you can make and receive calls and texts over Wi-Fi. ## 5. Emergency calls

VowiDroid cannot make emergency calls. When you dial an emergency number, the App hands the call to your phone's own dialer, which needs a mobile signal. A text to an emergency number may not get through. Do not rely on the App in an emergency. Where your carrier asks for an emergency address, the address you enter goes to the carrier, and keeping it correct is your responsibility.

## 6. Your information and privacy

The Developer does not run any server for the App and does not collect, receive or sell your data. The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

- The App keeps your texts, call history, settings and logs on your phone. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.
- To provide the service, the App exchanges information directly with your carrier: your SIM's identity (the IMSI, which the App encrypts where the carrier supports it), your phone number, the network you are on, and your calls and texts. It may also contact your carrier's entitlement server and download the carrier's encryption certificates. These exchanges are covered by your carrier's privacy policy.
- To find your carrier's Wi-Fi calling gateway, the App may look up the gateway's address through Google Public DNS (dns.google). That lookup sends the gateway's name, and Google sees your IP address.
- The App reads the contacts on your phone only to show names and photos, and never sends them anywhere.
- Logs can contain phone numbers and network identifiers. They leave your phone only if you share them.

## 7. Effect on your phone and line

The App runs its own Wi-Fi calling registration with your carrier. While it is on, your phone's built-in Wi-Fi calling for the same SIM should be turned off, and with some carriers your phone's own VoLTE may stop working. Calls and texts may be dropped, delayed or lost, and the service may stop working whenever a carrier changes its network. Android or your phone's own battery rules may stop the App in the background unless you allow it to run, as the Permissions page describes.
